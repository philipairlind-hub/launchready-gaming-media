Three factors explain why actual GTA VI downloads can take longer than ideal estimates. First, network load: Wi-Fi quality, household devices and provider congestion can reduce throughput. Second, download delivery: platform servers, pauses and updates affect transfer speed. Third, actual size: Rockstar says GTA VI may require up to 212 GB depending on device. Mbps means megabits per second, whereas MB/s means megabytes per second.
